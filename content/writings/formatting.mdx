## Headings and text

Body copy sits at a comfortable measure for long-form reading. It supports
**bold**, _italic_, `inline code`, and [links](https://saidev.codes). A longer
token such as `components/backdrop-shader.tsx` stays a single badge when it
wraps onto the next line.

### A third-level heading

Lists come in both kinds:

- An unordered item
- Another one, long enough to wrap so you can see how the line spacing holds up
  across more than a single line of text
- A third

1. First step
2. Second step
3. Third step
